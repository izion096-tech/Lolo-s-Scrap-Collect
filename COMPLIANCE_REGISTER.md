# Compliance register — implementation checklist

This checklist is not legal advice. The owner must obtain advice on the current consolidated South African laws, regulations and prescribed forms before use.

- Confirm business/dealer/recycler registration and any applicable SAPS requirements.
- Confirm current Second-Hand Goods Act and regulations, controlled metal rules, registers, retention periods, payment constraints, reporting duties and any Metal Trade System status.
- Verify required seller/counterparty identity and registration checks through an approved workflow; keep a verification outcome and method, not excess copies by default.
- Capture source/provenance declarations and authorisation evidence when required; capture vehicle details and burnt-cable explanations where applicable to the case/form.
- Apply separation of duties: intake operator cannot silently approve their own exception or edit an audit history.
- Reconcile scale ticket, gross/tare/net weights, material grade, agreed rate, transaction amount and bank/provider settlement.
- Define hold procedures for burnt cable, rail/infrastructure-like metals, batteries, hazardous e-waste, sealed items and disputed provenance.
- Confirm POPIA purpose, notice, access/correction, retention, security safeguards, operator agreements and compromise response.
- Confirm environmental, waste, transport, storage, fire-safety and municipal requirements for batteries and e-waste.
- Keep CSV export labelled as internal preparation only until mapped to the current prescribed format and checked by a competent person.
