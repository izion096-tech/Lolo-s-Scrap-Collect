# Material catalogue notes

The catalogue includes common ferrous and non-ferrous grades, copper alloys, cables, aluminium, stainless steel, motors, transformers, radiators, lead-acid and lithium-ion batteries, IT/e-waste, and PCB grades including server/telecom, desktop and laptop motherboards, RAM, ceramic/fibre CPUs, expansion cards, HDD controller boards, industrial control boards, power boards, TV/monitor boards, low-grade appliance boards, mobile boards, backplanes, gold-finger/edge connector boards and mixed PCBs.

No single universal South African grade list or rate card is assumed. Each buyer should configure its own acceptance specification, contamination deductions, sample/testing method, current rate, minimum lot size and rejected-material process. Demo prices are illustrative only and must not be treated as a quote.
